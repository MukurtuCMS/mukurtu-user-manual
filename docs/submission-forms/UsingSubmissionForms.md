# Using Submission Forms

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Authenticated users, Visitors

Submission forms let you submit content to a Mukurtu site without needing an account or community membership. For background on how they work, see [Understanding Submission Forms](UnderstandingSubmissionForms.md).

## Find a submission form

A site shares its submission form links with you directly, for example through an email, a printed flyer, or a link on another page. Each content type has its own submission form.

## Fill out the form

1. Under **Your contact information**, enter *Your name* and *Your email*. These fields are always required, even if you're logged in, so a reviewer can reach you about your submission.
2. If the form includes it, use the *How do you expect this item to be shared?* field to describe any access restrictions you'd like considered (for example, "only for my community" or "no restrictions"). This field is optional and helps the reviewer choose an appropriate sharing setting, since submitted content isn't assigned to a community or cultural protocol yet.
3. Fill in the remaining fields. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required; the rest depend on what the site has chosen to include on the form.
4. Select "Submit".

## After you submit

You'll land on a thank-you page confirming your submission was received. Your submission isn't published right away — it's held for a reviewer to check before it appears on the site.
