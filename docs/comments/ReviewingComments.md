# Reviewing Comments

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Mukurtu manager, Protocol steward, Language steward

When a comment requires approval, it's held from view until a reviewer approves it. There are two review queues, depending on your role.

## Review comments across the site

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Mukurtu manager

From your **Dashboard**, under **Notifications & Review**, select **Comment reviews**.

This page lists every comment on the site, split into **Published comments** and **Unapproved comments** tabs. From the **Unapproved comments** tab you can:

- Select "Approve" or "Delete" next to an individual comment.
- Select multiple comments, choose "Publish comment", "Unpublish comment", or "Delete comment" from the actions list, and select "Apply to selected items" to act on all of them at once.

## Review comments for the protocols you steward

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Protocol steward, Language steward

From your **Dashboard**, under **Notifications & Review**, select **Protocol comment reviews**.

This page gathers unapproved comments across every cultural protocol you steward into a single table, so you don't need to check each protocol separately. For each comment you can see its subject, a link to the content it was left on, its author, and the date it was posted, and select "Approve" or "Delete".

## Notifications

Stewards and managers are notified automatically when a new comment needs approval. Commenters are notified when their comment is approved or denied.
