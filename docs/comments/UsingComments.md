# Using Comments

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Authenticated users, Visitors

When commenting is open for an item, a comment form appears below its content.

## Leave a comment

1. Open the item you want to comment on and scroll down to the comments section.
2. If you're not logged in, enter *Your name*. Depending on the site's settings, you may also need to provide your *Email*, which is kept private and never shown publicly.
3. Enter your comment.
4. Select "Save".

## After you post

- If the site or the item's cultural protocol requires approval, you'll see a message explaining that your comment has been queued for review and will publish once approved.
- Otherwise, your comment appears right away.

## Edit your own comments

If you're logged in and have permission to edit your own comments, you can return to a comment you've posted and update it.
