# Create Community Records

!!! roles "User roles"
    Protocol steward, contributor

Community Records are an extension of digital heritage items that allow a greater range of information to be shared about a digital heritage item. They can be used for a number of different purposes, including:

   - Preserving an institutional record (such as from a library, museum, or archive) while enabling community members to add their own knowledge and context to the item
   - Allowing members of multiple communities to share their own information without having to erase or edit other community members' contributions
   - Sharing select metadata fields with different communities to help promote appropriate access to knowledge

Community records are created from digital heritage items and displayed together. They share the same media asset, but the rest of the metadata is completely independent. Community records can have different sets of protocols from the original digital heritage item.
