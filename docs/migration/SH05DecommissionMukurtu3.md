# Decommission Mukurtu 3 Site

When you are satisfied with with configuration and performance of the new Mukurtu 4 site, you can decommission the Mukurtu 3 site. The exact steps for this will depend on your hosting environment, but in general you will want to:

- Archive the Mukurtu 3 site directory and database for future reference. This is important in case you need to refer back to the old site for any reason.
- Remove the Mukurtu 3 site from your hosting environment. This may involve deleting the site directory and database, or simply disabling access to the site.
- Update DNS settings to point to the new Mukurtu 4 site. This may involve updating the A record or CNAME record for your domain, depending on your hosting environment.
- Update any links or references to the Mukurtu 3 site to point to the new Mukurtu 4 site. This may involve updating links in your site's content, as well as any external links that point to the old site.
- Notify users of the migration and provide any necessary instructions for accessing the new site.
