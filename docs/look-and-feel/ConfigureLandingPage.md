# Configure Landing Page

!!! roles "User role"
    Drupal administrator

Mukurtu 4 enables users to configure the landing page for their site to best showcase their content. Follow the directions below to configure the layout of your landing page.

1. From the **Dashboard**, navigate to the **Look and Feel** section and select the **Landing Page** link. 

    ![Screenshot of the look and feel section of the dashboard with the landing page link highlighted.](../_embeds/landing1.png)

2. This takes you to Mukurtu's **Layout Builder** editing form for the landing page. From here you can add or remove sections and blocks. 

3. To add a new section to your landing page, select the "Add section" button.
