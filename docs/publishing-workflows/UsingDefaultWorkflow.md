# Using the Default Content Workflow

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Mukurtu manager, Protocol steward, Language steward, Contributor, Curator, Language contributor

The default content workflow is the simpler of Mukurtu's two publishing workflows: anyone who can edit a piece of content can save it as a draft or publish it, with no separate review step. For an overview of its states and transitions, see [Understanding Publishing Workflows](UnderstandingPublishingWorkflows.md).

## Save content as a draft

While creating or editing content, select "Save as Draft" instead of "Publish" to save your work without making it visible to other users. Drafts are listed under your **Dashboard**, in **My Content > In Progress**, so you can find and continue them later.

## Publish content

When your content is ready to share, select "Publish" while creating or editing it. Published content becomes visible to everyone with access to its cultural protocols.

You can also publish an existing draft directly from **My Content**:

1. From your **Dashboard**, select **My Content**.
2. Find the item in the **In Progress** list.
3. Select "Publish" from the item's actions menu.

## Archive and restore content

!!! roles "User roles" 
    Mukurtu manager

Archiving removes published content from public view without deleting it.

- To archive published content, open the item and select "Archive".
- To bring archived content back as a draft, open the item and select "Restore to Draft".

## Bulk actions

From the content overview page, Mukurtu managers can select multiple items and apply "Publish", "Archive", or "Restore to Draft" to all of them at once.
